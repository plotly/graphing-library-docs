--- name: Setting an Explicit Tile Map View language: plotly_js suite: map-view order: 2 sitemap: false arrangement: horizontal markdown_content: | Passing `map.center` or `map.zoom` opts the subplot out of auto-fitting and opens it on exactly the view you give, even if that view is the default `zoom: 1` centered on `(0, 0)`.
