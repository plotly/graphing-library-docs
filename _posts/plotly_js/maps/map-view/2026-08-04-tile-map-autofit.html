--- name: Auto-Fitting a Tile Map to Data language: plotly_js suite: map-view order: 1 sitemap: false arrangement: horizontal markdown_content: | With no `center` or `zoom` given, a `map` subplot frames the trace data on the first render. It re-fits when the data changes — through `Plotly.restyle` of `lon`/`lat`, `Plotly.addTraces`, or `Plotly.deleteTraces` — until the user pans, zooms, rotates, or tilts the map, after which the chosen view is preserved.
